# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Technical Leads / Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction for a project. They translate product goals into sustainable engineering approaches and make sure architecture, technical risk, and delivery constraints are visible to the team.

### Responsibilities
- Set technical direction and guide architecture and engineering standards
- Lead technical design decisions and document material trade-offs
- Identify, assess, and mitigate technical risks, dependencies, and operational concerns
- Support Developers with design reviews, estimates, and implementation decisions
- Partner with Product Managers on feasibility, scope, and technical trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, secure, and scalable solutions
- Reduce technical uncertainty before and during delivery
- Keep technical decisions aligned with product outcomes and delivery commitments

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and engineering planning with Developers
- Risk and dependency updates with Project Managers
- Scope and trade-off discussions with Product Managers and QA

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers represent user needs through research, interaction design, visual design, and accessibility guidance. They make the intended customer experience clear before and during implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and define user experience requirements
- Create interaction flows, prototypes, and implementation-ready designs
- Define accessibility and design-system expectations
- Collaborate with Product Managers on customer needs and acceptance criteria
- Partner with Developers and QA to validate that delivered experiences match intended behavior

### Goals
- Deliver usable, accessible, and consistent customer experiences
- Reduce rework by clarifying design decisions early
- Validate that solutions address customer needs and product outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and backlog refinement with Product Managers
- Design handoffs and implementation reviews with Developers
- Usability and acceptance validation with QA and Customer Support / Operations Representatives

---

## Scrum Masters / Agile Facilitators

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Facilitators help teams use an effective delivery rhythm. They facilitate ceremonies, surface process impediments, and support continuous improvement without replacing the Project Manager's accountability for schedules, risks, and stakeholder communication.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team identify and remove workflow impediments
- Coach the team on agreed delivery practices and continuous improvement
- Track retrospective actions with the Project Manager and team owners
- Highlight process risks and capacity concerns for the Project Manager and Technical Lead

### Goals
- Maintain a sustainable, predictable delivery cadence
- Improve team collaboration and transparency
- Turn lessons learned into measurable process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Delivery ceremonies with Developers, QA, and Product Managers
- Blocker and capacity discussions with Project Managers
- Retrospective follow-up with all delivery roles and Stakeholders when needed

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors champion initiatives at the organizational level. They provide strategic direction, secure support for material decisions, and resolve escalations that exceed the delivery team's authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment, desired outcomes, and business priority
- Approve material scope, funding, or resource decisions when required
- Resolve escalated cross-team or business-impacting issues
- Review progress, risks, and decisions with Project and Product Managers
- Advocate for the initiative with senior Stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative remains aligned to organizational priorities
- Remove obstacles that cannot be resolved by the delivery team
- Enable timely decisions while preserving team-level ownership of day-to-day delivery

### Typical Communication
- Status, risk, and decision reviews with Project Managers
- Outcome and priority discussions with Product Managers
- Escalation discussions with relevant Stakeholders and Technical Leads

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Operations Representatives bring customer-impact and operational-readiness perspectives to delivery. They help teams prepare for releases, identify support-critical scenarios, and close the feedback loop after launch.

### Responsibilities
- Provide customer feedback, operational constraints, and support trends to Product Managers
- Identify support-critical scenarios, documentation needs, and readiness requirements
- Review release communications, known issues, and escalation paths
- Partner with QA on operational and support-focused validation
- Help monitor post-release customer impact and route feedback to the delivery team

### Goals
- Ensure releases are supportable and operationally ready
- Improve the team's response to customer issues and production feedback
- Reduce avoidable support volume through clear documentation and release communication

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback and release-readiness discussions with Product Managers
- Support scenario validation with QA and Developers
- Release communication and incident coordination with Project Managers and Stakeholders
