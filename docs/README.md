# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project Management Process Overview

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through an iterative, customer-first lifecycle for product features, services, and integrations. Teams begin by validating the business need, identifying stakeholders, defining measurable success criteria, assessing early risks, and deciding whether to proceed. Planning then turns approved work into a prioritized backlog of shippable increments, with estimates, a Definition of Done, dependencies, milestones, and a release plan.

Project Managers coordinate delivery plans, schedules, resources, risks, and cross-team communication. Product Managers define desired customer and business outcomes, prioritize the roadmap and backlog, and measure success. Developers collaborate on design, estimation, implementation, tests, and technical risk mitigation, while QA validates acceptance criteria and product quality. Stakeholders provide input, feedback, and approvals throughout the lifecycle.

Teams communicate through short daily standups, weekly delivery and PM/Product alignment meetings, sprint or milestone reviews, and regular stakeholder updates. A project board provides a shared view of work from backlog through review, QA, and completion. Teams maintain risk registers and escalate blockers from the delivery team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as needed.

Quality assurance is integrated into delivery through clear acceptance criteria, automated unit and integration tests, CI linting and security scans, peer-reviewed pull requests, and manual QA or end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows. Releases require completed acceptance criteria, passing checks, release notes, rollback readiness, staged deployment, post-deploy verification, and stakeholder communication. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents turn lessons into owned, time-bound improvement actions.
